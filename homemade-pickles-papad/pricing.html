Pricing Frequently Asked Questions

All prices on this website template are sample demonstration prices. When delivering to live clients, prices are adjusted based on prevailing market rates for raw mango crops, cold-pressed mustard oil, and logistics distance.

GST is included in retail consumer prices. Standard courier charges apply based on weight and destination pin code (free shipping is provided on domestic retail orders above ₹999).

We pack in shock-absorbent corrugated hive boxes with less than 0.2% transit damage history. In the rare event of transit damage, simply share an unboxing photo and we dispatch an instant free replacement without hassle.

We accept RTGS / NEFT bank transfers, UPI, credit/debit cards, and corporate purchase orders with 50% advance upon dispatch confirmation and 50% upon delivery.
