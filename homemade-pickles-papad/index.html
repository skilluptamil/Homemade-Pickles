Our Kitchen Story

Rooted in Tradition, Crafted with Mother's Care

Dadi's Swad was born in a sunlit ancestral courtyard in 1984. What started as grandmother packing hand-rolled urad dal papads and sun-cured raw mango achar in ceramic martabans for visiting grandchildren grew into a cherished household brand.

We preserve those sacred, unhurried methods: sun-drying green mango slices on clean muslin sheets, pounding whole spices on stone silbattas, and allowing the natural fermentation to unfold patiently under the golden afternoon sun.

Art of Preservation

Our Traditional Preparation Process

Four sacred steps passed down across generations that give our pickles and papads their unmistakable homely crunch and zest.

1

Careful Farm Sourcing

Only hand-inspected, firm raw mangoes, unblemished lemons, and heirloom garlic cloves enter our washing sinks.

2

Natural Sun-Drying

Diced produce is salted with crushed rock salt and sun-dried on terrace muslin sheets to eliminate all moisture naturally.

3

Stone-Ground Spicing

Mustard, fenugreek, fennel, and hing are gently dry-roasted on cast iron tawas and coarse-ground on traditional stone mills.

4

Barní Aging & Packing

Submerged in cold-pressed oil inside porcelain barnis for 21 to 40 days, stirred daily with neem wood paddles.
