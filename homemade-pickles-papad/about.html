Who We Are

Preserving Ancient Culinary Wisdom in Every Jar

Dadi's Swad is an artisanal Indian food kitchen dedicated to reviving authentic, sun-cured pickles, handmade papads, and stone-ground traditional condiments. We operate on the exact tenets our grandmothers lived by: whole farm-fresh ingredients, unrefined cold-pressed oils, pure natural rock salt, and unhurried solar aging.

In an era flooded with factory-produced preserves packed with synthetic acetic acid, artificial colors, and chemical stabilizers, we proudly stand for slow food. Every batch takes weeks of patient sun exposure to mature naturally.

100% Homemade

Hand-sorted, washed, cut, and spiced without industrial machinery.

FSSAI Certified

Certified clean hygienic facility meeting highest food safety benchmarks.

Our Heritage Journey

From Courtyard Martabans to Cherished Jars

Four decades of culinary passion, unbroken traditions, and unwavering devotion to taste.

1984 – The Ancestral Courtyard

Grandmother Sushila's Summer Pickling

Sushila Devi begins crafting raw mango and sun-cured kagzi lemon pickles for extended family and pilgrims passing through Varanasi.

2002 – Hand-Rolled Papads Introduced

Empowering Rural Women Artisans

Local homemakers join hands to roll traditional urad dal, pepper appalam, and steamed rice papads on stone rolling pins.

2018 – National Recognition & Gifting

Wedding Hampers & Diaspora Delivery

Expanding into festive gift boxes, wedding return gift hampers, and supplying overseas Indian diaspora seeking authentic tastes.

Present Day – Modern Standards, Ancient Heart

Over 50,000 Homes Enriched

Serving 14 distinct pickle variations and 6 crispy papad types nationwide while retaining 100% stone-ground preparation.

Purpose & Path

Empowering Traditions & Supporting Livelihoods

Our Mission

To protect and celebrate indigenous Indian food preservation techniques by delivering pure, unadulterated, preservative-free pickles and papads while empowering local women homemakers with dignified, sustainable craft livelihoods.

Our Vision

To make authentic, artisanal grandmother-style food heritage accessible across the globe, ensuring that future generations never forget the comforting taste of traditional sun-cured cooking.

Pure & Natural

Our Preparation Philosophy

Four sacred rules governing our kitchen that guarantee pure authenticity and wholesome digestion.

Cold-Pressed Oils

We use single-origin wooden kohlu (kachi ghani) mustard and til oils, retaining natural antioxidants and pungent warmth.

Unhurried Sun Cure

No artificially heated drying chambers. Our pickles absorb natural vitamin D and develop wild probiotic yeasts under open sunlight.

Ceramic Martabans

We exclusively mature our pickles in glazed porcelain and earthen pots that maintain ideal micro-temperatures without leaching chemicals.

Hand-Inspected Purity

Every raw mango, garlic clove, and amla fruit is visually inspected and hand-trimmed before entering the curing stage.
