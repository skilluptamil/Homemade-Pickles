Heritage Heritage

The Sacred Rhythm of Our Courtyard Kitchen

In traditional Indian households, pickle making was never a hasty chore; it was a festival. The entire family gathered as grandmothers judged raw mango acidity by scent, measured salt by palm weight, and tracked sun movement across the terrace.

Sun-Regulated Fermentation

We still time our batches according to summer heat, curing jars on open rooftop courtyards under direct sunlight.

Stone-Pounded Aromatic Oils

Crushing spices on slow stone mills preserves the essential volatile oils that modern high-speed electric blades burn off.
