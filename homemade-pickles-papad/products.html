Ingredients Sourced with Uncompromising Care

The secret to nostalgic pickle flavour isn't preservatives—it is the unadulterated freshness of raw ingredients. We source directly from native farming regions:

Kachi Ghani Mustard Oil

Cold-pressed from organic yellow & black mustard seeds.

Sun-Dried Guntur Chillies

Naturally rich red pigment and balanced pungency.

Pure Rock Salt (Sendha Namak)

Unrefined mineral salt preserving crisp vegetable texture.

Compounded Hing & Ajwain

Potent digestive herbs delivering deep culinary aroma.
